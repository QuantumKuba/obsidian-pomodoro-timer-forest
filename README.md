Built to motivate, not to nag. Missed days make the village sleepy, never broken, and nothing you build is ever taken away.

## Watch your tree grow

Press play and a seed is planted inside the timer. It grows into a tree as the ring fills. Finish and you harvest it; give up early and it withers.

## Something to aim for

Quests are for today; the **Village Charter** is for the months ahead. Your settlement climbs from Clearing to City through seven ranks, each earned by a short chapter of goals and rewarded with a landmark you can't buy. Along the way, four named neighbours pin small requests tied to real work, and their friendship unlocks permanent keepsake perks. Nothing expires, and ranks are never lost.

## Your Heart Tree

Some things can't be bought. Quests, the daily chest, neighbours' requests and charter chapters leave care items and ornaments glinting in your village for you to pick up. Choose one tree as your **Heart Tree**, meet its small daily need, and it grows from Young to Elder, stands taller, and wears the ornaments you've found. A missed day costs nothing; it simply waits.

## Made for how you work in Obsidian

- ✅ **Tick a task, earn a reward.** Any `- [ ]` task in your vault. [Rewards](docs/GUIDE.md#rewards)
- 🌿 **Done early? Harvest early.** Finish the task, keep most of the reward. [How](docs/GUIDE.md#done-early-harvest-early)
- 🏷️ **Smart seeds.** `#write` plants a Cherry Blossom, `#code` a Pine. [Tags](docs/GUIDE.md#smart-seeds-tags)

- 📓 **Daily-note logging.** Dataview-friendly lines for every session. [Logs](docs/TIMER.md#log)
- 🌲 **The Grove journal.** Any past day as a small island of trees. [Grove](docs/GUIDE.md#the-grove-journal)

## Quick start

1. **Install.** Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` into `<your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/`, then enable the plugin under *Settings → Community plugins*.
2. **Open it.** Click the timer icon in the ribbon. The trees icon opens the full-page village.
3. **Press play.** Your first tree (Classic Pine) is free, and you start with a couple of saplings to plant.
4. **Keep going.** The [user guide](docs/GUIDE.md) explains rewards, quests, perks and streaks.

## Privacy & your data

No accounts, no network requests, no telemetry. Your progress lives in one file in your vault, `.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/data.json`, and the plugin only touches your notes for features you turn on. Sync that file to take your village to another device, and back it up before you uninstall: removing the plugin deletes it. Details: [permissions](docs/GUIDE.md#permissions-and-privacy), [several devices](docs/GUIDE.md#using-more-than-one-device), [uninstalling safely](docs/GUIDE.md#uninstalling-without-losing-your-progress).
