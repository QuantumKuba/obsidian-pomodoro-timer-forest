## 🧠 Why it works so well for ADHD brains

Standard Pomodoro timers can feel like rigid taskmasters: stressful countdowns, jarring alarms that snap you out of deep flow, and punishing streak counters that wipe out weeks of hard work after one rough day. For neurodivergent minds, that often leads straight to shame, dread, and quitting the app altogether.

**Pomodoro Timer Forest was designed from the ground up to support how ADHD brains actually work:**

* **👁️ Visual time anchoring (Cures time blindness):** Abstract numbers like `24:59` can either spark anxiety or blur into background noise. Forest replaces timer anxiety with a living metaphor: watch a seed sprout, leaf out, and grow into a full tree as you focus. You can *feel* time passing visually.
* **⚡ Instant dopamine feedback loops:** ADHD brains crave immediate reward signals. Rather than just an anti-climactic alarm sound, every completed session celebrates your effort: harvest **☀️ Sunlight**, **🪙 Coins**, and **XP** to expand your homestead, level up, and unlock rare flora.
* **🌊 Hyperfocus Mode ("Ride the wave"):** When intense hyperfocus kicks in, rigid 25-minute cutoff alarms are infuriating. Hyperfocus Mode rolls your session into an open-ended count-up journey with **zero wither risk**, gentle hydration check-ins, and breadcrumb notes to eliminate re-entry paralysis.
* **🛡️ Guilt-free streaks (No shame spirals):** Missed a day or had a tough week? Your village simply takes a nap under gentle mist. No trees die, no buildings vanish, and your streak never resets to zero in shame. One single session wakes your villagers right back up.
* **🌿 Done early? Harvest early:** In an ADHD focus sprint, you might finish a task in 12 minutes instead of 25. Don't sit idle or feel penalized—tick the task off, choose *Harvest early*, collect 75% of your rewards, and keep your momentum intact.
* **🎯 Frictionless task initiation:** Overcoming starting paralysis is half the battle. Click play directly on any `- [ ]` markdown task in your notes or Kanban board to anchor your attention immediately.
* **🎧 Distraction-free ambient audio:** Built-in synthesised soundscapes (gentle rain, stream, breeze) mask environmental noise right inside Obsidian—no browser tabs, YouTube ads, or Spotify rabbit holes.

## 🌊 Hyperfocus: ride the wave

When you find yourself in the zone, rigid timers shouldn't hold you back. **Hyperfocus Mode** is tailored for ADHD flow states, giving you flexible scaffolding without breaking your concentration:

* **Ride the wave:** When a standard Pomodoro finishes, smoothly transition straight into Hyperfocus with one click—no alarm bells, no reset.
* **Zero wither guarantee:** Stopping or resetting a Hyperfocus session is always treated as a successful landing, never a failure. If you focused for at least 10 minutes, you harvest your tree, earn full rewards, and collect your sapling.
* **Gentle body check-ins:** Every 30 minutes, a soft chime or gentle banner reminds you to take a sip of water, relax your jaw, and stretch your shoulders. Acknowledging check-ins earns bonus coins!
* **Landing & breadcrumb note:** When landing, write a quick "Where was I?" note (e.g., `Next: finish testing the login route`). The note is automatically logged under your active task, eliminating post-focus re-entry paralysis when you return.
* **Fair pacing & recovery breaks:** Full rewards for the first 90 minutes, tapering gently afterward to discourage unhealthy marathons, followed by a suggested recovery break to recharge your cognitive battery.

## Something to aim for (The Village Charter)

Daily quests keep today exciting; the **Village Charter** gives you meaningful long-term motivation. Your settlement climbs through seven historic ranks—from a humble Clearing to a thriving City—earned through achievable chapters of focus goals. 

Along the way, four friendly neighbours move into your village, offering bite-sized requests tied to your real work. Befriending them unlocks permanent keepsake perks. Best of all: **nothing ever expires, and ranks are never lost.**

## ⚡ Quick start

1. **Install:** Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` into `<your-vault>/.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/`, then enable the plugin under *Settings → Community plugins*.
2. **Open the panel:** Click the **timer icon** in the ribbon to open the sidebar. Click the **trees icon** to open the full-page village view.
3. **Press play:** Your first tree (Classic Pine) is free, and you begin with starter saplings to plant right away.
4. **Grow & thrive:** Check out the complete [User Guide](docs/GUIDE.md) to explore buildings, perks, quests, and hyperfocus tips.

## 🔒 Privacy & your data

* **100% Local & Offline:** No accounts, no subscriptions, no servers, and zero network telemetry.
* **Vault-Native:** All game progress is saved locally in `.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/data.json`.
* **Sync Friendly:** Sync this file via Obsidian Sync, Git, or iCloud to seamlessly continue your village on other devices.
* **Backups:** Remember to back up `data.json` before uninstalling if you want to keep your village progress.

For full details, see the guide sections on [Permissions &amp; Privacy](docs/GUIDE.md#permissions-and-privacy), [Multi-device Sync](docs/GUIDE.md#using-more-than-one-device), and [Safe Uninstalling](docs/GUIDE.md#uninstalling-without-losing-your-progress).
