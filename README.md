A Pomodoro timer for Obsidian where every session grows a tree, and every tree goes into a small village floating in the sky. Your projects get islands of their own, joined to home by bridges you build with focus.

It's made for brains that tune out a plain countdown and give up on apps that punish a bad week. Progress is something you can see, rewards land the moment a session ends, and nothing you build is ever taken away.

## Why it works for ADHD brains

Most Pomodoro apps assume you'll stick to the schedule. This one is built around the days you don't.

- **Time you can see.** The tree grows with the countdown, so 25 minutes is something you watch happen instead of a number you ignore.
- **Rewards right away.** Sunlight, Coins and XP the moment a session ends, with every bonus listed.
- **Hyperfocus mode.** When the timer ends mid-flow, keep going. It counts up instead of cutting you off, never withers your tree, nudges you to drink water every 30 minutes, and lets you leave a "where was I" note for later.
- **Streaks that sleep instead of breaking.** Miss a few days and the village naps under a bit of mist. One session wakes it up. Nothing is lost.
- **Done early? Harvest early.** Finished in 12 minutes? Tick the task off and keep 75% of the reward.
- **Start from the task.** Press play on any `- [ ]` task or Kanban card, so starting is one click.

## Install

In Obsidian, open **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**, search for **Pomodoro Timer Forest**, then install and enable it. Or [open it in the plugin directory](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest).

Then click the timer icon in the left ribbon and press play. Your first tree is free, and you start with a few saplings to plant. The [user guide](docs/GUIDE.md) covers the rest.

<details>
<summary>Manual install</summary>

Download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the [latest release](https://github.com/QuantumKuba/obsidian-pomodoro-timer-forest/releases/latest), put them in `<your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/`, and enable the plugin under *Settings → Community plugins*.

</details>

## Privacy

No account, no server, no tracking. Everything stays in your vault, in `.obsidian/plugins/pomodoro-timer-forest/data.json`. Sync that file with Obsidian Sync, Git or iCloud to carry your village between devices, and back it up before uninstalling if you want to keep it. More in [permissions and privacy](docs/GUIDE.md#permissions-and-privacy) and [using more than one device](docs/GUIDE.md#using-more-than-one-device).
